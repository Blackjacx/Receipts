# Pound Cake (Loaf Tin)

## Ingredients
    400 g           vegetable margarine
    300 g           sugar
    200 g           flour
    150 g           chopped nuts
    7               eggs
    1 packet        baking powder

## Optional ingredients
    200 g           raisins
    100 g           candied orange peel
    2 packets       orange zest
                    chopped chocolate pieces

## Preparation
Beat the soft butter with the sugar and vanilla sugar until fluffy. Then add the eggs one at a time and mix well. Add the flour mixed with the baking powder and all the other ingredients and stir the batter until nice and creamy. Then pour it into the greased tin and bake at 180 degrees for approx. 1 hour. Skewer test - if nothing sticks, the cake is done.

## Duration
approx. 1 h 20 min
