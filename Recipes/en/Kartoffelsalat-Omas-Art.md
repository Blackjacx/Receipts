# Grandma's Potato Salad

## Ingredients
    500 g           potatoes, boiled in their skins
    3-4             onions, medium
    2               apples, peeled
                    gherkins
    1 tub           Fleischsalat, German meat salad (alternatively mayonnaise or salad cream)
                    salad oil
                    herb vinegar
    some            granulated stock
    1 tbsp          mustard
                    salt
                    pepper
                    sugar

## Preparation
Boil the potatoes in their skins the day before, then peel them and slice them thinly or cut them into small cubes. Slice the apples and gherkins thinly or dice them, and chop the onions as finely as possible. Pour hot stock over the potatoes, just enough for them to absorb the liquid and no more. Add the sliced ingredients. Season with the spices, oil and vinegar. A little linseed oil gives the salad a special touch.

## Duration
approx. 30 min preparation<br />
Boil the potatoes 1 day ahead
