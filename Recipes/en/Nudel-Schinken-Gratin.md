# Pasta and Ham Gratin

## Ingredients
    400 g           pasta of your choice
    140 g           ham
    500 ml          water
    500 ml          cream (Rama cooking cream / milk works too)
    1 tbsp          flour
    1-2 tsp         salt
    1/2 tsp         pepper, or more to taste
    1 tsp           paprika (sweet or hot)
    1 pinch         sugar
    1               garlic clove
    1/2             onion
    250 g           cheese: Cheddar or Emmental or Gouda or Tilsiter

## Preparation
Put the uncooked pasta into an ungreased baking dish. Cut the cooked ham into small pieces (strips or squares) and mix it into the pasta.

### Sauce
Chop the garlic and onion finely and whisk them with the other ingredients, except the cheese, in a cold pot. Bring everything to the boil and let it simmer for 2-3 minutes. Then pour it all over the pasta and bake in the oven at approx. 180 degrees Celsius (fan) for 15 minutes. Now spread the cheese over it and put it back in the oven for another 15 minutes.

Careful, hot! And enjoy your meal 🙃

## Duration
45 min
