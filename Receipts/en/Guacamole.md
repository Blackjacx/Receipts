# Guacamole

## Ingredients
    1           avocado, medium-sized and medium-ripe
    1/2         tomato
    1           onion, small, red
    1 tbsp      coriander leaves
    1           lime
    1/2         jalapeño
                sea salt and freshly ground pepper

## Preparation
Peel the avocado and keep the stone for garnishing later. Cut the avocado into small pieces and put them into a bowl. Squeeze the lime and pour the juice over the avocado. Add salt and pepper and mash everything with a fork.

Finely chop the tomato, onion, coriander and jalapeño and add them to the mash. Mash everything briefly once more and garnish with the avocado stone.

## Duration
30 min
