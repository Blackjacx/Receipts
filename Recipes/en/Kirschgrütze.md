# Cherry Pudding (Kirschgrütze)

## Ingredients
    1 jar           morello cherries
    2 tablespoons   sugar
    250 ml          cherry juice
    1 packet        no-cook gelling agent for fruit
    4 tablespoons   lemon juice
    250 ml          whipped cream
    4 tablespoons   chocolate shavings
    4               rolled wafers

## Preparation
Stir the sugar into a little cherry juice, then stir in the gelling agent, the lemon juice and the rest of the cherry juice. Carefully fold in the cherries and fill into small glass bowls.

Chill the pudding well and garnish generously with the whipped cream, chocolate shavings and rolled wafers.

## Duration
15 min
