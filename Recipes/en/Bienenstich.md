# Bee Sting Cake

## Ingredients
### Dough
    200 g           butter
    200 g           sugar
    375 g           flour
    3 small tsp     baking powder
    3               eggs
    small packet    lemon zest
    1 pinch         salt

### Topping
    100 g           butter
    100 g           sugar
    100 g           almonds, chopped
    100 g           desiccated coconut
    1 tbsp          honey
    2 tbsp          milk


## Preparation
### Dough
Cream the butter, sugar, lemon zest and salt. Stir in the eggs. Add the flour and baking powder. Spread the dough evenly on a baking tray.

### Topping
Melt the butter and add the sugar and honey. Add the almonds and coconut and finally the milk. Spread over the dough.

Bake at 180° for 30-40 min.

## Duration
1 h
