# Ginger Lime Mint Iced Tea

## Ingredients
    2           organic limes
    5           tea bags, green or black tea
    1 pc        organic ginger, a palm-sized piece
    4.5 l       water (5 l pot)
    2 tbsp      sugar, white
    5           mint sprigs

## Preparation
Bring the water and sugar to the boil.

Wash the limes, halve them and squeeze out the juice. Throw the peels into the pot.

Simmer everything over medium heat for 10 minutes. Take it off the heat and let the tea bags and mint steep in it for 10 minutes. Then remove the tea bags and mint. Let the brew steep and cool for 3h.

Meanwhile, mix the lime juice with the grated ginger in a bowl. Add a good ladle of the tea and let it steep as well until the tea is lukewarm. Then add it to the tea and let it steep for another 3h. Now pour everything through a sieve into bottles rinsed with hot water.

Fill glasses halfway with ice cubes and top up with the lime ginger tea.

## Duration
30 min preparation<br />
1 night cooling time
