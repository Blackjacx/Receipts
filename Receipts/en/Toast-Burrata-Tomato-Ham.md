# Toast with Burrata, Tomato and Ham

## Ingredients for 2 servings
    1               burrata (125 g)
    4 slices        Parma ham (approx. 60 g)
    8               cherry tomatoes
    10 leaves       basil
    1 tbsp          olive oil
    2               bread rolls or 1/2 baguette
                    salt
                    pepper

## Preparation
**Mixture:** Put the burrata on a chopping board and pull it apart with two forks. Add the Parma ham, basil leaves and cherry tomatoes, season with salt and pepper and drizzle with olive oil. Chop everything together with a large knife until you have a coarse, spreadable mixture.

**Bread:** Cut the roll open lengthways and toast it until golden brown.

**Serving:** Pile the mixture generously onto the bottom half, put the lid on and serve immediately.

## Duration
10 min
