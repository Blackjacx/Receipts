# Chicken Sandwich

## Ingredients for 2 servings

      6 slices      wholemeal bread
      2             eggs, boiled
      8 slices      sliced chicken breast
      1             tomato
      2 leaves      iceberg lettuce
    1/2             cucumber
      2 tbsp        paprika spread, light

## Preparation

- Spread the bread with the paprika spread
- Wash the lettuce leaves, wash the cucumber and tomato and slice them
- Top 2 slices of bread with cucumber, tomato, a lettuce leaf and sliced chicken breast
- Slice the boiled egg and add it to the sandwich as well
- Stack the two topped slices on top of each other, close the sandwich with the third slice and hold it together with a wooden skewer
- Season with salt and pepper

## Duration
- 30 min
