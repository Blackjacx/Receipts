# Cream Cheese Roll

## Ingredients
    200 g           herb cream cheese
    1 medium        onion
    1 packet        Lachsschinken (cured pork loin), thinly sliced
    1 small         courgette
    600 g           yeast dough

## Preparation
Roll out the yeast dough. Spread the herb cream cheese thinly over it. Top with thinly sliced onion rings. Lay the thinly sliced Lachsschinken on top. Slice the courgette thinly and lay the slices across the rolling direction.

Roll up the dough and press the end down. Cut the roll into slices approx. 3 cm thick.

Bake on the middle shelf at 180 degrees, conventional (top and bottom heat), for 15-20 min.

## Duration
45 min
