# Pasta with Caramelised Onions

## Ingredients for 4 servings
    4 large         onions (approx. 800 g)
    2 tsp           paprika, sweet
    2 tsp           Italian herbs
    80 g            sun-dried tomatoes, without oil
    4 tbsp          olive oil
    1 bulb          garlic, separated into cloves
    100 g           baby spinach
    1/2 bunch       parsley
    400 ml          coconut milk
    400 g           pasta (e.g. farfalle)

## Preparation
**Roasted vegetables:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C conventional (top and bottom heat) or 180 °C fan. Slice the onions into rings and put them into a baking dish. Season with paprika and Italian herbs, cut the sun-dried tomatoes into strips and add them together with the olive oil, then mix everything well. Place the whole garlic cloves on top, cover the dish with aluminium foil and bake for 1 hour until the onions are soft and caramelised.

**Sauce:** Take the dish out of the oven and remove the foil. Stir in the spinach, chopped parsley and coconut milk until the spinach wilts and a creamy sauce forms.

**Pasta:** Cook the pasta separately, drain it and fold it into the sauce until it is completely coated.

## Duration
1 h 15 min
