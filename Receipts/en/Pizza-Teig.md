# Pizza (Dough)

## Ingredients
### Dough
    150 ml          water
    2 tbsp          olive oil
    1/2             yeast cube / alternatively: dried yeast
    300 g           flour type 0
    1/2 tsp         salt
    1 pinch         sugar

### Topping
    1 clove         garlic
    1/2 tube        tomato purée
    300 g           passata / alternatively: ready-made pasta sauce, e.g. with basil
    1               mozzarella ball
                    oregano and thyme

## Preparation
- Warm the water, mix it with the sugar and dissolve the yeast in it - leave for 10 minutes
- Mix the flour, yeast water, oil and salt
- Knead for approx. 10 minutes until the dough comes away from your hands and the bowl, adding flour as needed. It's easiest to loosen the dough from the bowl and your hands with a silicone spatula.
- Preheat the oven to 50 °C and leave it open for a few seconds
- Now put the dough into the oven for 1h, covered with a linen cloth. Alternatively: leave it to rise in the fridge for 1 day, covered.
- After rising, "roll out" the dough thinly on the oven rack lined with baking paper and leave it to rise again for approx. 30 min, so air gets into the dough and it becomes "fluffy".
- Mix the passata, tomato purée, garlic clove(s) and any other spices to taste and spread it on the dough. It's important that the mixture is moist enough so the dough doesn't dry out later while baking.
- Top the pizza as you like
- Preheat the oven to 250°C (as hot as possible) and bake on the lowest shelf for 5 min.

## Duration
1 h 30 min - 1 day, depending on how tasty you want the dough to be
