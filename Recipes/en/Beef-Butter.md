# Beef Butter

## Ingredients
                    beef shank slices
                    seasoning to taste
                    smoking chips
                    barbecue sauce
                    beef stock
                    butter

## Preparation
Season the beef shank slices to taste. Put smoking chips into the grill and smoke the shank slices over indirect heat at 140°C for 2 hours.

Put the browned shank slices into a dish together with the barbecue sauce, beef stock and small pieces of butter. Cover with aluminium foil and put back on the grill at 150°C for another 2 hours.

Tip: Turn it into burgers.

## Duration
4 h
