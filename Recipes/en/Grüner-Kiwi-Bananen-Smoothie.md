# Green Kiwi Banana Smoothie

## Ingredients
    1 bunch             radish greens (unsprayed or organic)
    1 medium            banana, ripe
    1 medium            kiwi, ripe
    150 ml              orange juice, freshly squeezed
    1 tablespoon        lime juice, freshly squeezed
    50 ml               mineral water, still
    1 teaspoon          oil (linseed oil)

## Preparation
Wash the radish greens thoroughly, shake them dry and chop them. Peel the banana and the kiwi and cut them roughly into pieces. Blend everything together with the water, orange juice, linseed oil and lime juice until smooth.

## Tip
Radishes are very healthy and contain, among other things, mustard oil, which gives them their slightly hot taste and has an antibacterial effect. Radish greens are said to be even more nutritious than the radishes themselves, so they shouldn't simply be thrown away.

## Duration
10 min
