# Pasta and Vegetable Bake

## Ingredients for 6 servings
    500 g           pasta
    2 stalks        leeks
    2               red peppers
    1/2 bunch       parsley
    4               eggs
    125 g           cheese, grated
    200 ml          cream
    250 g           cherry tomatoes
                    pepper
                    salt
                    fat for the dish

## Preparation
Cook the pasta in boiling salted water for 10 minutes.

Wash the leeks and cut them into thick rings. Three minutes before the end of the cooking time, add the leeks to the pasta, then drain.

Halve the tomatoes, cut the peppers into strips and chop the parsley finely.

Stir the eggs with the cheese and cream and season with salt and pepper. Mix all the ingredients in a large bowl and spread them on a greased baking tray.

Bake in the preheated oven at 200°C for approx. 30 minutes.

## Duration
50 min
