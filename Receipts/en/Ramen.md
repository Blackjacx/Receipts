# Ramen

## Ingredients for 4 servings
### Broth
    2-3 tbsp        red curry paste
    3 cloves        garlic
    20 g            ginger
    800 ml          coconut milk
    1 tbsp          agave syrup
    2 tbsp          fish sauce
    2 tbsp          soy sauce
    1               lime
    4 nests         ramen noodles

### Toppings
    400 g           minced meat
    2               eggs
    4               spring onions
    4 tbsp          crispy fried onions
    1/2 bunch       coriander
                    chilli flakes

## Preparation
**Broth:** Fry the red curry paste in a wok until fragrant. Add finely chopped garlic and ginger and fry briefly. Pour in the coconut milk, stir in the agave syrup, fish sauce and soy sauce and let the broth simmer for a few minutes. Finally add the zest of one lime and season to taste.

**Noodles:** Put the ramen noodles straight into the broth and cook them in it until they come apart and are soft.

**Toppings:** Boil the eggs for 6-7 minutes until soft in the middle, rinse them under cold water, peel and halve them. Brown the minced meat until crumbly. Slice the spring onions into fine rings and chop the coriander roughly.

**Serving:** Fill deep bowls with noodles and broth. Top with minced meat, half an egg each, spring onions, crispy fried onions, coriander and chilli flakes.

## Duration
30 min
