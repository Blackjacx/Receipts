# Coconut Macaroons

## Ingredients for 4 servings
    5           egg whites
    375 g       icing sugar
    500 g       desiccated coconut
    50 pcs      wafer papers (Ø 40 mm)
                icing sugar for dusting

## Preparation
Whisk the egg whites with a hand mixer and slowly sprinkle in the sifted icing sugar. Gradually fold 450 g of desiccated coconut into the egg white foam. Place the coconut mixture on the wafer papers and sprinkle with the remaining desiccated coconut.

Line a baking tray with baking paper. Place the macaroons on the tray and bake in the preheated oven at 160°C for approx. 20 min. Then let them cool and dust with icing sugar.

## Duration
40 min
