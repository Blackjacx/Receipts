# Leek Bake with Salami and Cheese

## Ingredients
    2 stalks        leeks
    500 g           boiled potatoes
    200 g           salami, sliced
    200 ml          whipping cream
    200 g           grated cheese
                    salt
                    pepper
                    oregano
                    butter

## Preparation
Wash the leeks and cut them into thin rings. Grease a baking dish with butter and put the leek rings in it. Dice the potatoes and add them to the dish as well. Cut the salami into squares and add it to the baking dish. Sprinkle salt and pepper over the ingredients, going easy on the salt, as the salami and cheese are already salty. Mix the ingredients carefully. Pour the cream over the bake. Bake in the preheated oven at 175°C for 25 minutes. Take the bake out, sprinkle it with the grated cheese and scatter oregano over it. Finish baking in the oven for another 20 minutes.

## Duration
45 min
