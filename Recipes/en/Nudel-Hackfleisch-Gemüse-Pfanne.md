# Pasta, Mince and Vegetable Skillet

## Ingredients
    500 g           minced meat
    150 g           short-cut spaghetti
    400 g           passata
    400 ml          water
    1 stalk         leek
    1 small         courgette
    1               red pepper
    2               carrots
    2 cloves        garlic
                    salt
                    pepper
                    rosemary
                    thyme
                    Italian herbs
                    olive oil

## Preparation
Cut the courgette, carrots and leek into thin slices and the pepper into small cubes. Brown the minced meat in oil, season it, add the vegetables and braise everything together for approx. 10 minutes. Then add the passata and water. Crush the garlic and add it. Now stir in the spaghetti and herbs. Let it cook for 7-10 minutes, then season again with salt and pepper.

## Duration
40 min
