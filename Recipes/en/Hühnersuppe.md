# Chicken Soup

## Ingredients
    1 bunch     soup greens (3 carrots, 1 piece of celeriac, 1 small leek, parsley)
    1/2 kg      boiling chicken
    2           medium onions
    2           bay leaves
    3 berries   allspice
    125 g       soup noodles
                salt
                pepper

## Preparation
First remove the giblets from the chicken, if not already done. Then rinse the chicken with cold water.

Put the bird into a large soup pot and fill it 3/4 with water. Add the following, uncut: 1 medium carrot, half of the celeriac, 1 onion, the bay leaves and the allspice berries. Add plenty of salt and pepper and boil everything for 40 min. Add water if too much boils away.
Meanwhile, chop the remaining vegetables as you like.

When the chicken is done, pour everything in the pot through a sieve, e.g. the kind used for draining pasta or rice.
Then return the broth to the pot.
Take the meat off the bones and cut it into small pieces.
The cooked vegetables, the bay leaf and the allspice berries are no longer needed.

Now put the meat and broth back into the pot together with the chopped vegetables and the noodles. Boil for another 10-15 minutes. Season with salt and pepper and add the parsley.

Without the vegetables, you get a very tasty, clear chicken broth that can be eaten with noodles, rice or simply on its own.

## Duration
1 h 25 min
