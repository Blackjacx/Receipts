# Minced Meat Tray Bake

## Ingredients
    1 ½ kg          minced meat
    3-4             eggs
                    onion
                    breadcrumbs
    3-4             peppers
    1-2 tins        mushrooms
    2 tubs          cream
    2 tubs          Schmand (thick sour cream)
    2 sachets       hunter's sauce mix (small)

## Preparation
Prepare the ingredients above as you would for meatballs, and chop the peppers and mushrooms. Hold back some of the peppers and mushrooms, mix everything else into the mince and press it onto an ungreased baking tray with a high rim. Let it stand for a while, then spread the rest of the peppers and mushrooms on top.

Before baking, stir the cream, Schmand and hunter's sauce mix together and spread it over the mince layer. Bake it in the oven for approx. 30-40 min.

## Duration
40 min
