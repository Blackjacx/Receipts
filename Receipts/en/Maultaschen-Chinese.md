# Maultaschen Chinese Style (Sichuan Chilli Oil)

## Ingredients
                    Maultaschen (Swabian filled pasta pockets)
                    garlic, crushed
                    chilli powder
                    sesame seeds
                    oil for frying
                    Sichuan peppercorns
                    soy sauce
                    rice vinegar
                    chicken stock powder
                    sugar
                    pak choi
                    spring onions
                    salt

## Preparation
Bring water to the boil, add the Maultaschen and reduce the heat to medium. Meanwhile, press the garlic into a deep plate and add the chilli powder and sesame seeds. Heat a little oil in a pan, add the Sichuan peppercorns and pour the hot oil over the plate. Add the soy sauce, rice vinegar, salt, chicken stock powder and sugar and stir well.

Add the pak choi to the pot and cook for another 5 minutes. Lift the Maultaschen and vegetables onto the plate with a slotted spoon, mix well with the sauce and garnish with spring onions.

## Duration
20 min
