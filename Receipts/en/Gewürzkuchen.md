# Spice Cake

## Ingredients
	250 g           flour
	250 g           sugar
	250 g           margarine
	4               eggs
	1 teaspoon      cocoa
	1 sachet        vanilla sugar
	1 sachet        baking powder
	3 tablespoons   chocolate sprinkles
	125 ml          red wine
	1 sachet        spice mix

## Preparation
Beat the margarine, eggs and sugar until creamy. Mix the flour, spice mix, vanilla sugar, cocoa, chocolate sprinkles and baking powder, add them and stir in. Now stir in the red wine. Before filling the tin, grease it with margarine and sprinkle it with breadcrumbs. Bake at 150°C for approx. 60 min.

## Duration
1 h 30 min
