# Sesame Roast Potatoes with Coriander Mint Quark

## Ingredients (4 servings)
    1 kg            new potatoes, waxy
    2 tablespoons   olive oil
    1 teaspoon      coarse sea salt
    2 tablespoons   sesame seeds
    500 g           quark 20 % fat
    75 g            sour cream 10 % fat
    1/2 bunch       coriander
    1/2 bunch       mint
    2               spring onions
    2 tablespoons   sparkling mineral water
                    salt
                    freshly ground pepper

## Preparation
Wash the potatoes thoroughly, halve them lengthways and pat them dry. Mix the olive oil and salt in a bowl and toss the potatoes in it. Spread them on a baking tray lined with baking paper, bake in the preheated oven at 180°C for approx. 30 min depending on their size, sprinkle with sesame seeds and finish cooking in another 15 min.
Mix the quark and sour cream with the chopped herbs and spring onions. Stir in the mineral water. Season the herb quark with salt and pepper to taste. Serve with the roasted potato halves.

## Duration
1 h
