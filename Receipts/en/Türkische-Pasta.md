# Turkish Pasta (Yalanche Mante)

## Ingredients
                    minced meat
    1               onion, diced
    1 tbsp          butter
    1 tbsp          tomato purée
                    pasta water
                    pasta
                    natural yoghurt
    1-3 cloves      garlic, grated
                    butter
                    paprika
                    dried mint
                    salt

## Preparation
Brown the minced meat in a pan and salt it. Add 1 tbsp of butter and 1 tbsp of tomato purée and fry them together with the mince. Add the diced onion and a little pasta water. Cook the pasta at the same time.

Stir the natural yoghurt with the grated garlic cloves. In a small pan, fry butter together with paprika and dried mint.

Arrange the pasta on a plate, spread the mince over it, top with the garlic yoghurt and drizzle the paprika butter over it.

## Duration
15 min
