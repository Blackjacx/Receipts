# Pesto Genovese

## Ingredients

    1 bunch     basil
    2 tbsp      pine nuts or sunflower seeds
    3 tbsp      Parmesan, freshly grated
    1/2 tbsp    balsamic vinegar, white
    100 ml      olive oil
    3 cloves    garlic
    1/2 tsp     salt
                pepper

## Preparation

Toast the pine nuts as much as possible without letting them turn black, then let them cool.

Pick the basil leaves off the stems and discard the stems.

Now crush everything in a mortar and finally work it into a creamy paste by adding the olive oil drop by drop.

Finally season with salt and pepper.

## Duration

10 min
