# Pizza Quesadilla

## Ingredients
                    pepperoni salami
                    mozzarella
    1               egg
    1               tortilla
                    hot chilli oil
                    salt
                    pepper

## Preparation
Put the salami into a hot pan and spread the mozzarella over it. Whisk the egg with salt and pepper and pour it into the pan. Spread everything evenly. Lay the tortilla on top and wait approx. 60 seconds. Flip, fold over, drizzle with a little hot chilli oil and serve.

## Duration
5 min
