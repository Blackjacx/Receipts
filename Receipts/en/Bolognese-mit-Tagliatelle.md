# Bolognese Sauce with Tagliatelle (4 Servings)

## Ingredients
    300 g           beef
    150 g           pancetta
    50 g            carrots
    50 g            celery sticks
    50 g            onions
    20 g            tomato purée
    1/2 glass       red or white wine
    1 glass         whole milk
                    salt

## Preparation
1. Finely chop the onions, carrot and celery
1. Heat oil in a large pot and sweat the vegetables in it
1. Cut the pancetta and beef into small cubes
1. Fry the pancetta with the vegetables for approx. 10 minutes
1. Add the beef and brown it over high heat
1. Deglaze with the wine to intensify the flavour (milk can be used to mellow the flavour)
1. Add the tomato purée
1. Season with salt
1. Simmer for 3-4h, stirring now and then
1. Cook the tagliatelle - add 20 g of salt per l of water
1. Toss the pasta with the sauce. If the sauce doesn't cling, a splash of pasta water will fix that.

## Duration
approx. 4 h
