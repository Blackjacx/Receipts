# Ramen Mongolian Beef

## Ingredients
                    beef strips, thinly sliced
                    salt
                    white pepper
    1               egg white
                    bicarbonate of soda
                    cornflour
                    oil for deep-frying
                    garlic, chopped
                    spring onions
                    brown sugar
                    soy sauce
                    water
                    ramen noodles

## Preparation
Slice the beef thinly and season it with salt, white pepper, egg white, bicarbonate of soda and cornflour. Marinate for 30 minutes. Deep-fry in hot oil for approx. 30 seconds and take out.

In a hot wok, fry the garlic and the white parts of the spring onions. Add the brown sugar, soy sauce and water and bring to the boil. Thicken with cornflour mixed into cold water. Return the beef to the wok and mix in the ramen noodles and the green parts of the spring onions.

## Duration
45 min
