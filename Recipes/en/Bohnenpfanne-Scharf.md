# Spicy Bean Skillet à la Bud Spencer & Terence Hill

## Ingredients for 4 servings
    2 tins          kidney beans (400 g each)
    1 tin           white beans (400 g)
    3 large         onions
    4               garlic cloves
    1               red pepper
    1               chilli (as brave as you are)
    2 tbsp          tomato purée
    400 g           chopped tomatoes (tinned)
    2 tbsp          olive oil
    1 tsp           cumin
    1 tsp           smoked paprika
                    salt, pepper

## Preparation
1. Finely chop the onions, garlic, pepper and chilli.
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onions, garlic and chilli until translucent.
1. Add the pepper and fry for another 2 minutes. Stir in the tomato purée.
1. Add the tinned tomatoes and stir everything well.
1. Drain and rinse the beans and add them to the pan. Season with cumin, paprika, salt and pepper.
1. Leave everything to simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring now and then.

## Tip
Control the heat with the amount of chilli – from mild to "Bud Power". Serve with crusty bread, polenta or rice. The bean skillet tastes even better the next day – just like a good spaghetti western.

## Duration
approx. 35 min
