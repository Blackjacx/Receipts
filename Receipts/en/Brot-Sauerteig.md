# Sourdough Bread

## Sourdough starter

### Ingredients

                    wholemeal rye flour
                    water

### Preparation

Every day for 5 days, mix 50g of flour with 60ml of water and keep the starter covered at 28 degrees. After day 5, feed the starter once a week and store it in the fridge.

### Duration

At least 5 days to get an active starter.

## Bread dough

### Ingredients

    500 g           flour (80% light spelt flour type 630 / 20% wholemeal wheat flour)
    300 ml          lukewarm water
    100 g           active sourdough starter
    1 tsp           salt

### Preparation

#### Easy

Mix everything lightly. Wrap it in a linen cloth and leave it to rest in the fridge for 12-24h. Fold it several times in between.

Score the bread lengthways. Put a dish of water at the bottom of the oven so the bread rises better. Place the bread on a baking tray above it. Put another tray over it to make the baking space smaller (similar to a Dutch oven). Now bake in the oven at 230°C for 45 minutes.

#### Advanced

Mix the starter with the same amount of flour and water and leave it covered at room temperature for 4h.

After 3h, prepare the main dough. Mix the spelt flour, wheat flour, salt and remaining water and set it, covered, next to the other dough.

After 4h, knead both doughs together well and leave to rise, covered, at room temperature for another 7h. During those 7h, fold the dough every hour to develop a good gluten structure. After 7h, shape the dough into a round on a work surface and let it rest for 10 min. Meanwhile, prepare the proofing baskets (a bowl lined with a linen cloth).

Now build tension in the loaf. Rub flour over the surface and turn the loaf over. Pull and stretch the bottom part and fold it to the middle. Stretch the left part and fold it over the whole loaf. Do the same with the right and top parts. Now fold and twist the top, middle and bottom together and roll the loaf up from bottom to top. Flour the top, place it upside down in the proofing basket, cover and leave in the fridge overnight.

Preheat the oven to 250°C with a cast-iron pot inside. Then score the bread lengthways. Put the bread into the cast-iron pot and bake for 15 minutes. Then take the bread out of the pot and finish baking on the rack for approx. 30 minutes, until it has a nice dark colour.

### Duration

#### Easy

Preparation/resting time: 12-24h
Baking time: 45 min

#### Advanced

Preparation/resting time: 20h
Baking time: 45 min

## Duration

5 days for the starter<br />
approx. 1 day for the bread
