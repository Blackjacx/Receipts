# Croquetas de Jamón (Spanish Ham Croquettes / Tapa)

## Ingredients (4 servings)
### Béchamel sauce
    400 ml          milk
    2 tbsp          flour
    30 g            butter
                    salt, pepper
                    nutmeg

### Also
                    oil or butter for frying
                    flour for dredging
    100 g           breadcrumbs
    250 g           Serrano ham
    1               onion
    3               eggs

## Preparation
Cut the ham into very small cubes.

Crack one egg and separate the white from the yolk. Set the white aside for later. Mix the ham well with the yolk plus one more egg.

Melt the butter in a small pan or saucepan. Slowly whisk in the flour and sweat it until golden. Add the milk, stir until smooth and bring briefly to the boil. Then cook over medium heat for 10 minutes, stirring constantly. Add the ham and onions and cook for another 5 minutes, stirring constantly. Season with salt, pepper, lemon juice and a little nutmeg.

Take the sauce off the heat and spread it on a large, flat plate (ideally a pizza plate). Let it cool, then cover it with cling film and put it in the fridge for half a day.

Now whisk the last egg with the egg white you set aside, and set out flour, egg and breadcrumbs in three deep plates. Using a wet tablespoon, scoop portions from the ham and béchamel mixture, shape them into croquettes and coat them first in flour, then in egg and finally in breadcrumbs.

Heat plenty of oil or butter in a deep pan and fry the croquettes in batches. Drain briefly on kitchen paper and serve very hot.

## Duration
Working time: 45 min <br />
Resting time: 1/2 day
