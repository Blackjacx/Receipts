# Carrot Curry Soup

## Ingredients for 2 servings

    300 g           carrots
      1             potato, medium-sized, floury
      1             apple, small
      1             onion
    2-3 cloves      garlic
    500 ml          vegetable stock
    200 ml          coconut milk, reduced fat
  1/2 - 1 tsp       curry paste
  1 piece           ginger
                    iodised salt
                    pepper

## Preparation

- Wash and core the apple
- Peel the carrots and potato and dice them together with the apple
- Peel the onion, garlic and ginger and dice them finely
- Heat 1 tbsp vegetable oil in a pot and sweat the onions
- Add the potato, apple, ginger and curry paste and roast them for 3-4 min, then add the carrots
- Deglaze with the vegetable stock and coconut milk, simmer over medium heat for approx. 20 min until soft and season with salt and pepper

## Duration
- 45 min preparation time
