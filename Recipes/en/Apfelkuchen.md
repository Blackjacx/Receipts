# Apple Cake

## Ingredients
    125 g           margarine
    50 g            butter
    125 g           sugar
    200 g           flour
    750 g           apples
    3 tablespoons   milk
    2 teaspoons     baking powder
    1 packet        grated lemon zest
    1 packet        vanilla sugar
    3               eggs

## Preparation
Mix all the ingredients. Put 2/3 of the batter into a springform tin and cover it with apple slices. Stir the milk into the remaining batter and spread it over the apples. Dot the apples with flakes of butter and sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon. Bake at 180°C for approx. 45 min.

## Duration
1 h
