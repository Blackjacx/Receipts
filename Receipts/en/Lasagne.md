# Lasagne Bolognese

## Ingredients
### Bolognese sauce
                    olive oil
    500 g           minced meat
    1               onion
    2               garlic cloves
    1 bunch         parsley
    2               bay leaves
    1 tbsp          tomato purée
    1 tin           chopped or puréed tomatoes

### Béchamel sauce
    1/2 l           milk
    30 g            butter
    40 g            flour
                    salt, pepper
                    lemon juice
                    nutmeg

### Also
    300 g           lasagne sheets
    1 bag           grated cheese

## Preparation
### Bolognese sauce
Dice the onions finely and chop the garlic finely. Heat the olive oil in a pot and brown the minced meat in it. Add the chopped onions, parsley, garlic, bay leaves and tomato purée and fry briefly. Then pour in the chopped tomatoes and season with salt and pepper. Let it reduce for approx. 30 minutes with the lid slightly open (without a lid it spatters a lot).

### Béchamel sauce
Melt the butter in a small pan or saucepan. Slowly whisk in the flour and sweat it until golden. Add the milk, stir until smooth and cook over medium heat for 10 minutes, stirring constantly. Season with salt, pepper, lemon juice and a little nutmeg.

### Lasagne
Spread a little Bolognese sauce in a buttered, ovenproof dish, lay a layer of lasagne sheets on top and cover the pasta with Bolognese sauce and then with a layer of béchamel. Then another layer of pasta, Bolognese sauce and béchamel. Fill the dish layer by layer like this.
The last layer should be béchamel sauce; sprinkle it thickly with grated cheese. Bake the lasagne at 180 °C fan for approx. 30 - 40 minutes until the crust is golden brown.

#### Tip
The lasagne can easily be prepared a day ahead and left to rest in the fridge.

## Duration
Working time: 30 min <br />
Cooking time: 1h
