# Lentil Feta Salad

## Ingredients
                    red lentils
                    cucumber
    1               tomato
                    feta
                    onion
    ½               lemon
                    parsley (frozen)
                    salt
                    pepper
                    chilli

## Preparation
Cook the red lentils in salted water for 9 minutes. Meanwhile, cut the cucumber, tomato, feta and onion and put them into a bowl. Squeeze half a lemon over it. After cooking, rinse the lentils well and add them to the vegetables. Sprinkle with parsley, salt, pepper and chilli, and it's ready.

## Duration
15 min
