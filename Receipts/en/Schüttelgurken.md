# Shaken Cucumber Pickles

## Ingredients
    2 long          cucumbers
    2 medium        onions
    3 tablespoons   sugar
    2 teaspoons     salt
    2 teaspoons     mustard seeds
    2 teaspoons     dill
    4 tablespoons   vinegar 10%

## Preparation
Slice the cucumbers and cut the onions into rings. Put the sugar and mustard seeds into a container. Then layer the cucumbers and onion rings on top of each other in the container. Finally add the salt, vinegar and dill to the jar and season with pepper. The cucumbers need to sit in the fridge for at least 24h to infuse properly. After two days they taste really good.

## Duration
15 min preparation<br />
24 h infusing time
