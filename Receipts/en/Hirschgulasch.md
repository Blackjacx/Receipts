# Venison Goulash

## Ingredients
    1 kg        venison
    4           onions
    400 g       mushrooms
    3 tbsp      clarified butter for searing
    2           bay leaves
    3           juniper berries
    1/2 bulb    celeriac
    2 stalks    leeks
    3           carrots
    2 bottles   red wine
    2 tubs      buttermilk
    1 l         game stock
    4 tbsp      flour
    4 tbsp      Rama Creme Fine (cooking cream)
    1 pinch     salt
    1 pinch     freshly ground pepper
    1 tbsp      tomato purée
    1 dash      red wine vinegar
    1 jar       lingonberries
                salt and pepper

## Marinating the meat (24-48h)
First cut the venison into bite-sized pieces. Trim the vegetables and chop them roughly. Then bring them to the boil for 5-10 minutes together with the bottle of red wine, a little pepper, the juniper berries and the bay leaves. Let the marinade cool. When it is lukewarm, add the buttermilk and put the venison in it.

## Final preparation
Pour off the marinade, as it can no longer be used because of the buttermilk. Then separate the meat from the vegetables. Rinse both with cold water to remove the buttermilk residue.

You now need 3 pots. The first is for searing the meat over high heat. To speed up the searing, regularly pour off the liquid that collects into a bowl (it will be added back later). In the second pot, bring the second bottle of red wine and the rinsed vegetables briefly to the boil once more. Briefly bring the game stock to the boil in a third pot as well. We will use both liquids later to deglaze and season the meat.

Add the onions and mushrooms to the seared meat and fry them briefly. Then deglaze with 2/3 of the red wine liquid (the vegetables can be discarded) and 2/3 of the game stock. Never deglaze the meat with cold liquid. Add the red wine vinegar and the juices collected during searing. Stir in the tomato purée. Now simmer the meat with the lid on over medium heat for approx. 2h, stirring now and then. In the meantime, the remaining game stock can be used to strengthen the flavour of the goulash and the red wine liquid to soften it, to get the flavour just right.

Finally, mix the flour well with the Rama Creme Fine, stir it into the goulash together with 2/3 of the lingonberries and bring it briefly to the boil once more.

Put the remaining lingonberries into a small serving bowl and stir in a little hot water. This makes the lingonberries nice and smooth, and they can be served as a side with the goulash.

## Duration
24–48 h marinating<br />
30 min preparation<br />
2 h 30 min cooking time
