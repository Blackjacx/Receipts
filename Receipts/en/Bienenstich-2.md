# Bee Sting Cake with Filling


## Ingredients for the dough
```
500 g           flour
1 cube          fresh yeast (42 g)
1/4 l           lukewarm milk
100 g           sugar
1 pinch         salt
100 g           butter or margarine
1               grated zest of one lemon
```

## Ingredients for the topping
```
200 g           butter or margarine
200 g           flaked almonds
200 g           sugar
2 packets       vanilla sugar
2 tbsp          milk
```

## Ingredients for the filling
```
45 g            cornflour
2               egg yolks
1/2 l           milk
100 g           sugar
2 packets       vanilla sugar
1 pinch         salt
100 g           butter or margarine
1/4 l           cream
```

## Preparation
Put the flour into a bowl, press a well into the middle and crumble in the yeast. Stir it with a little of the lukewarm milk, one tsp of sugar and a little flour into a starter dough, cover and leave to rise in a warm place for about 15 minutes.

Dissolve the butter or margarine in the rest of the lukewarm milk and add it to the flour together with the remaining sugar, a pinch of salt and the grated lemon zest, then knead everything into a smooth dough. Keep kneading until the dough comes away from the side of the bowl. Add a little more flour if needed. Cover and leave to rise in a warm place for 30 minutes.

Knead the risen dough briefly once more, roll it out on a baking tray lined with baking paper and leave it to rise, covered, for another approx. 30 minutes.

For the topping, melt the butter or margarine in a pan, add the flaked almonds, sugar, vanilla sugar and milk and bring everything to the boil once.

Spread the cooled but still warm mixture over the dough and bake at about 200 °C for 20 minutes.

For the filling, stir the cornflour and egg yolks together with a little milk. Bring the rest of the milk to the boil with the sugar, vanilla sugar and salt, stir in the cornflour mixture and bring back to the boil. Leave to cool, stirring often.

Beat the soft butter or margarine until fluffy and stir in the cooled pudding a spoonful at a time. Whip the cream until stiff and fold it into the cream mixture.

Cut the cooled cake into pieces, slice each piece in half horizontally, spread the bottom half with the cream and put the top back on.

Chill in the fridge for a few hours before serving.

## Duration
approx. 1 h 45 min
