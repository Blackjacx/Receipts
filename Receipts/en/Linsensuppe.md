# Lentil Soup

## Ingredients
    400 g           lentils
    400 g           potatoes
    50 g            smoked bacon
    1               onion
    1               garlic clove
    1 tsp           curry powder
    2 tbsp          vegetable stock
    2               bay leaves
                    salt
                    pepper
                    sugar
                    vinegar
                    nutmeg

## Preparation
Wash the lentils and let them soak overnight in plenty of water.

Sweat the diced bacon, finely diced onions and finely chopped garlic until translucent.

Now cook the lentils with the soaking water, one extra litre of water, the finely diced potatoes and all the other ingredients for 30 minutes.

Season to a sweet-and-sour taste with salt, pepper, a pinch of sugar and nutmeg, marjoram and vinegar, and serve.

## Duration
- 1 night preparation time
- 1 h cooking time
