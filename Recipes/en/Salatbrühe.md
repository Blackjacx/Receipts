# Simple Salad Dressing

## Ingredients
    1 tbsp      sugar
    3 tbsp      water
    1 tsp       salt
    300 ml      water
                vinegar
                onion
                chives / shallots
                dill

## Preparation
Finely chop the onions and dill, and slice the chives / shallots into rings. The salt and sugar need to be dissolved in the water. Then add the vinegar. Add the herbs and pour everything over the salad.

## Duration
10 min
