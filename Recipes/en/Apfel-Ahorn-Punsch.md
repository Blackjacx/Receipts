# Apple Maple Punch

## Ingredients
	1 medium            apple
	3 bags              fruit tea with apple/cinnamon flavour
	1/4 litre           apple juice
	1                   cinnamon stick
	2                   star anise
	4                   cloves
	4 tablespoons       maple syrup

## Preparation
Wash the apple and slice it very thinly, skin and all. Heat the fruit tea, 1/2 litre of water, the apple juice, the spices and the apple slices in a pot and let them steep over low heat for 10 min. Stir in the maple syrup and pour the hot punch into glasses.

## Duration
15 min
