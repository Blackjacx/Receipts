# Potato Balls

## Ingredients
    500 g           potatoes
                    cheese, grated
                    chives
                    cornflour
                    oil for deep-frying
                    Parmesan, grated
                    chilli
                    parsley

### Chilli mayo
                    mayonnaise
                    chilli

## Preparation
Peel the potatoes, cut them into small pieces and boil them. Drain and mash. Mix with the cheese, chives and cornflour and knead well. Shape into small balls. Deep-fry in hot oil and then sprinkle with Parmesan, chilli and parsley.

Serve with a chilli mayo.

## Duration
approx. 45 min
