# Original Carbonara

## Ingredients
    4               egg yolks
                    guanciale
                    Pecorino Romano
                    Parmigiano Reggiano
                    spaghetti
                    black peppercorns
                    salt

## Preparation
**Guanciale:** Unwrap the guanciale and cut off the rind. Cut the meat first into thick slices, then into strips and finally into cubes. Put the cubes into a cold pan and render them slowly over medium heat until golden brown and crisp. Pour the rendered fat through a fine sieve into a small bowl and set the crispy cubes aside.

**Pepper:** Toast the peppercorns in a dry pan until fragrant, then crush them coarsely in a mortar.

**Sauce:** Finely grate the Pecorino and Parmigiano. Separate the eggs and put the yolks into a bowl; the whites are not needed. Add the cheese and the coarsely crushed pepper, mix in the still-warm guanciale fat and stir everything into a thick, smooth paste.

**Pasta:** Cook the spaghetti al dente in plenty of boiling salted water. Set the bowl with the paste on a folded kitchen towel so it doesn't slide. Away from the heat, gradually stir in hot pasta water until a glossy, creamy sauce forms. Add the drained spaghetti and toss until completely coated. Finally mix in the crispy guanciale.

**Serving:** Twirl the spaghetti into a nest with tongs and place it on the plate. Pour the remaining sauce over it and sprinkle with guanciale, grated cheese and pepper.

## Duration
30 min
