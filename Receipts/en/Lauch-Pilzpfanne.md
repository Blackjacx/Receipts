# Leek and Mushroom Pan

## Ingredients
    300 g           stale baguette or ciabatta
    1 stick         leek
    300 g           shiitake
    300 g           brown mushrooms
    6 sprigs        rosemary
    2               garlic cloves
    6 tablespoons   olive oil
    6 tablespoons   white balsamic vinegar
    2 tablespoons   lemon juice
                    salt
                    freshly ground pepper

## Preparation
Cut the baguette into slices and put them into a large bowl. Rinse the rosemary, shake it dry, strip the needles from two sprigs and chop them finely. Halve the leek lengthways, wash and trim it and cut it into thin slices. Clean the shiitake and brown mushrooms and slice them; peel the garlic and chop it finely.

Put 1-2 tablespoons of oil into a hot pan and sear the mushrooms in it over high heat. Mix in the garlic and leek. Fry over medium heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally. Season with salt, pepper and rosemary, take off the heat and let cool a little.

Mix the remaining oil with the vinegar, lemon juice, sugar, salt and pepper. Add the vegetables and the dressing to the bread, toss loosely and leave to infuse for at least 30 min. Then season to taste and serve garnished with rosemary.

## Tip
If you like your bread a little crispy, you can also toast the slices under the oven grill for 2-3 min.

## Duration
1 h
