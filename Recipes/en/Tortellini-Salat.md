# Tortellini Salad

## Ingredients for 1 serving

    250 g           tortellini
     50 g           cocktail tomatoes
      1 tub         mozzarella balls
      1 tbsp        olive oil
      1 handful     basil
     50 g           grated Parmesan
                    iodised salt
                    pepper

## Preparation

- Cook the tortellini according to the packet instructions
- Wash and halve the tomatoes
- Drain the mozzarella and halve it if you like
- Chop the basil finely
- Put all the ingredients into a bowl and mix with the olive oil
- Season with salt and pepper

## Duration
- 30 min preparation time
