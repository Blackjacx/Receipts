# Carbonara Ramen

## Ingredients for 2 servings
    2 packets       instant ramen (Buldak), with sauce and seasoning sachets
    80 g            processed cheese (approx. 4 slices)
    100 g           diced bacon
    400 ml          milk
    20 g            butter
                    black pepper

## Preparation
**Bacon:** Heat the wok until very hot and melt the butter in it. Add the diced bacon and render it until it has taken on some colour.

**Sauce:** Lay the cheese slices on the bacon and let them melt. Pour in the milk and stir into a creamy sauce.

**Noodles:** Add the uncooked ramen block to the sauce and cook it until it comes apart and the noodles are soft. Then stir in the sauce sachet and the seasoning powder.

**Seasoning:** Add plenty of freshly ground black pepper and toss everything vigorously over high heat until the noodles are completely coated in the sauce. Serve immediately.

## Duration
10 min
