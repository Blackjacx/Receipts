# Thuringian Apple Cookies

## Ingredients
    100 g               margarine
    125 g               sugar
    1 packet            vanilla sugar
    2 teaspoons         lemon zest, grated
    1 pinch             salt
    2                   eggs
    250 g               flour
    1/2 packet          baking powder
    2 small             apples
    1 cl                rum, or 3 drops of rum flavouring
    1 knife tip         cinnamon
    1/2 packet          candied lemon peel
    1/2 packet          candied orange peel

## Preparation
Stir together the margarine, sugar, vanilla sugar, lemon zest and salt.

Stir in the eggs. Mix 200 g of the flour with the baking powder and stir it in.

Grate the apples coarsely, then mix them with the rum, the remaining flour and the cinnamon. Then mix them into the dough.

Using two teaspoons, place small heaps on a baking tray lined with baking paper. Leave enough space between them. Bake in the oven preheated to 200°C for 18 minutes.
Dust with icing sugar and serve, ideally warm.

The cookies don't turn hard and crumbly like typical cookies, but stay soft and cake-like.

## Duration
1 h
