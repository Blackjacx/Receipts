# Waffle Batter

## Ingredients

    250 g           flour
    3/8 l           sour cream
      3             egg yolks
      3             egg whites
     40 g           sugar

## Preparation

- Sift the flour into a bowl
- Stir it with the sour cream, egg yolks and sugar into a smooth batter
- Whisk the egg whites until stiff and fold them into the batter
- Heat the waffle iron
- Add 2 tablespoons of batter at a time and bake for 2 minutes
- Serve with vanilla sugar, cinnamon sugar, jam or compote, to taste

## Duration
- 30 min preparation time
