# Vegetable Stock

## Ingredients
    3 l         water
    3 large     carrots
    2           onions
    1 stick     leek
    1/2 bulb    celeriac
    1/2 bulb    fennel
    2           parsnips
    1           beetroot
    4           garlic cloves (large)
    3           bay leaves
    5           peppercorns

## Preparation
Wash all the vegetables and cut them roughly into pieces. Put the vegetables into a pot large enough to still cover everything with water. The pot should hold approx. 6l of water. Now cover the vegetables with water and let everything boil for over an hour. Then strain off the vegetables and season the stock with salt and pepper. You can now do the following with the stock:
- Freeze it in ice cube trays and use it as needed
- Keep it in the fridge and use it up within about a week

To finish, chop fresh vegetables and cook them in the stock with noodles or your favourite side.

## Duration
2h
