# Thai Red Curry

## Ingredients
    250 ml      coconut milk
    1/4 pc      palm sugar
    10 g        curry paste (1/4 packet)
    50 g        chicken
    1 tsp       grated ginger
    1 tbsp      soy sauce
    4           small broccoli florets
    1/4         pepper
    1/2         small carrot
    4           pea pods
    20 g        Thai basil
    2 bunches   coriander
    4           makrut lime leaves
    1           small chilli
    1 stalk     lemongrass
    1 stalk     spring onions
                rice

## Preparation
Cut the pepper into rough pieces, the carrots into strips and the spring onions into slices, chop the coriander and basil finely and peel the lemongrass.

Bring the coconut milk to the boil. Now add the makrut lime leaves, lemongrass and ginger. Let it simmer for 5 minutes. Then stir in the curry paste, palm sugar and soy sauce. Let it simmer for another 5 minutes.

Cook the carrots briefly, then add all the other vegetables and cook for 2 minutes over medium heat.

Season to taste, done.

## Duration
1 h
