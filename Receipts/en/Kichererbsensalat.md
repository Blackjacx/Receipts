# Chickpea Salad

## Ingredients: salad
    400 g           chickpeas, dried
    1               cucumber
    250 g           cherry tomatoes
    3               spring onions
    1 bunch         parsley

## Ingredients: dressing
    1-2             garlic cloves
    5 tbsp          lemon juice
    1 tbsp          honey
    1/2 tsp         cumin, ground
    1 tsp           salt
    3/4 tsp         pepper
    6 tbsp          olive oil

## Preparation
Soak the chickpeas in cold water overnight. Then pour off the water and rinse the chickpeas. Wash the cucumber, cherry tomatoes and spring onions. Halve the cucumber lengthways and halve both halves again. Cut into thin slices.

Halve the cherry tomatoes and slice the spring onions into rings. Wash the parsley, pat it dry, pick off the leaves and chop them roughly. Put the vegetables and parsley into a large bowl.

Peel and crush the garlic clove. Put it into a bowl with the lemon juice, honey, cumin, salt and pepper and stir. Add the olive oil and stir until everything has combined into a creamy dressing.

Pour the dressing over the salad. Mix everything and optionally season with lemon juice, salt and pepper. Serve and enjoy!

## Duration
Working time 15 min <br />
Preparation time 24h
