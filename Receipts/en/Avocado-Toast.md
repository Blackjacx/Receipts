# Avocado Toast

## Ingredients
### Base
    1-2             avocados, ripe
                    bread (sourdough or white), thickly sliced
                    butter
                    olive oil
                    sea salt, coarse
                    freshly ground pepper

### For the variations
                    eggs
                    chilli flakes or chilli oil
                    spring onions
                    pesto
                    cherry tomatoes, red and yellow
                    tomatoes, ripe or green
                    crème fraîche
                    caviar
                    red onion
                    chives

## Preparation
### Base
Cut the bread into thick slices and toast them in butter until golden, in a pan or on the grill. Halve the avocado lengthways, remove the stone and scoop the flesh out of the skin with a spoon. Slice the halves thinly, fan them out by hand and lay them on the bread. Alternatively, mash the avocado with salt and pepper into a cream and spread it on the bread. Finally drizzle with olive oil and season with sea salt and pepper.

### Variations
**Fried egg:** Place a fried egg with a runny yolk on the avocado fan, drizzle with chilli oil or sprinkle with chilli flakes, and scatter with spring onion rings.

**Poached egg:** Top with a poached egg, drizzle with olive oil and season with chilli flakes and pepper.

**Scrambled egg:** Spoon creamy scrambled egg onto the avocado, add a dollop of crème fraîche and garnish with caviar, finely diced red onion and snipped chives.

**Boiled egg:** Lay slices of green tomato on the avocado fan, top with a peeled soft-boiled egg and sprinkle with chilli flakes, red onion and sea salt.

**Pesto and tomatoes:** Spread the bread with pesto, lay the avocado fan on top and cover with roasted red and yellow cherry tomatoes.

**Tomato:** Spread the bread with avocado cream, top with thick tomato slices and season with olive oil, sea salt and pepper.

## Duration
10 min
