# Cheese and Leek Soup

## Ingredients
    480 g           lean minced beef (tartare)
    2               onions
    1.5 l           beef bouillon
    6 stalks        leeks
    2 tbsp          flour
    75 g            processed cheese triangles (25 % fat)
    240 g           herb cream cheese (30 % fat)
                    salt
                    pepper
                    sweet paprika

## Preparation
Brown the minced beef in a non-stick pan without fat. Dice the onions and sweat them with it. Top up with the bouillon and add the leeks, cut into rings.

Stir 2 tbsp of flour into 1/2 cup of cold water and then add it slowly to the soup while stirring.

Add the cheese and let it simmer for about 20 minutes. Season with salt, pepper and paprika.

## Duration
40 min
