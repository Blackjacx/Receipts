# Sambal Matah

## Ingredients
    4                   shallots • sliced into fine rings
    4                   bird's eye chillies • sliced into fine rings
    2 cloves            garlic • thinly sliced
    1 leaf              makrut lime (kaffir lime) • cut into strips
    1/4 teaspoon        dried shrimp paste (toasted terasi) • crumbled
    1 stalk             lemongrass • use only the white, tender part • crush and slice into fine rings
    1 pinch             salt
    1/2 teaspoon        freshly squeezed makrut lime juice
    1 teaspoon          coconut oil, extra virgin

## Preparation
Mix all the ingredients and serve as a side.

## Duration
15 min
