# Potato Salad

## Ingredients
    600 g           potatoes, boiled in their skins
    2               apples
    1               green and orange pepper
    10              gherkins
    2               shallots
    300 g           cooked ham
    1 punnet        cress
    2 tablespoons   chopped chives and parsley
    8 tablespoons   apple cider vinegar
    8 tablespoons   walnut oil
    2 teaspoons     sweet mustard
                    salt
                    freshly ground pepper
                    sugar

## Preparation
Boil the potatoes in their skins, let them cool a little and peel them. Wash the apples, remove the cores and cut them into small pieces. Cut the gherkins, pepper, ham and potatoes into small pieces. Slice the shallots into rings. Mix everything together with the cress, herbs, vinegar, oil and mustard, season to taste and leave to infuse for a little while.

## Duration
approx. 45 min
