# Cheesecake

## Ingredients

### For the base

    200 g           flour
    75 g            sugar
    75 g            margarine
    1               egg(s)
    ½ packet        baking powder
                    fat for the tin

### For the filling

    125 g           margarine
    225 g           sugar
    1 sachet        vanilla sugar
    1 sachet        vanilla pudding powder
    3               egg(s)
    500 g           quark
    300 g           yoghurt

## Preparation

Put the ingredients for the base into a bowl, knead them together and line a greased 26 cm springform tin with the dough. Pull it up the side by about 2 - 3 cm.

For the filling, stir the margarine, sugar, vanilla sugar, pudding powder and eggs together in a bowl. Then mix in the quark and yoghurt.

Preheat the oven to 180 °C.

Now pour the filling into the tin, smooth it and bake in the hot oven for 30 min (fan) / 45 min (conventional, top and bottom heat).

Note: Only take the cake out of the tin once it has cooled completely, as the quark filling is still too soft right after it comes out of the oven.

## Duration

Working time approx. 30 min and cooking/baking time approx. 45 min
