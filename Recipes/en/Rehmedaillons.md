# Roe Deer Medallions

## Ingredients

    500 g       potatoes (floury)
    300 g       celeriac
    800 g       saddle of roe deer
    8-10        thyme sprigs
    250 g       blackberries
    2-3         shallots
    500-700 g   porcini mushrooms
    1 tbsp      coriander seeds
    100 ml      milk
    100 g       butter
    50 g        whipping cream
    70-100 ml   aged brandy
    200 ml      port
    200 ml      game stock
    1-2 tbsp    blackberry jelly
    50 g        dark chocolate
    10 g        nut nougat
                salt
                pepper
                vegetable oil
                rosemary sprigs

## Preparation
Peel and wash the potatoes and celeriac, cut them into large pieces and cook them in a pot of salted water for approx. 20 min until soft. Drain and let the steam evaporate.

Preheat the oven to 120°C (fan 100°C). Rinse the saddle of roe deer, pat it dry and remove the sinews. Set the sinews aside. Pick the thyme leaves. Wash and clean the blackberries. Peel the shallots and dice them finely. Clean the porcini. Lightly crush the coriander seeds.

Season the saddle with salt and pepper, heat oil in a pan and sear the meat in it over high heat. Then sprinkle it with the thyme leaves, put it on a baking tray and cook it in the preheated oven for 15 min.

Meanwhile, heat the milk, butter and cream in a pot, press the potatoes and celeriac through a potato ricer and fold them into the warm milk mixture. Season with salt and pepper and keep warm.

## Duration
1 h
