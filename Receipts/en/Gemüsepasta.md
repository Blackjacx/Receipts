# Vegetable Pasta

## Ingredients
    1               courgette
    1               red pepper
                    broccoli florets
                    cherry tomatoes
    1               onion
                    herb cream cheese
                    olive oil
                    pasta
    1 ladle         pasta water
                    Parmesan, grated
                    salt
                    pepper

## Preparation
Put the courgette, pepper, broccoli, cherry tomatoes and onion into a baking dish. Place the herb cream cheese in the middle and drizzle with olive oil. Roast in the oven at 180°C for 30-40 minutes. Cook the pasta in the meantime.

Take the roasted vegetables out of the oven, put everything into a blender, add a ladle of pasta water, season with salt and pepper and blend until smooth. Pour the sauce over the pasta and serve with Parmesan.

## Duration
45 min
