# Pumpkin Soup

## Ingredients
    1 kg            Hokkaido, butternut or other squash
    1 l             water
    3               carrots
    3               potatoes
    4               onions
    4 cloves        garlic
    1 piece         ginger
    250 ml          coconut milk
                    soup vegetables (no leek)
                    winter vegetables (parsnip, beetroot, …)
                    pepper, Thai curry, cumin, sweet paprika
                    marjoram to taste

## Preparation
Peel all the vegetables and dice them roughly. Then sear them in a pot over high heat. Now add the water and let it boil for 30 min. Season with salt and spices.

Let everything cool down, add fresh ginger and blend until creamy.

## Duration
45 min
