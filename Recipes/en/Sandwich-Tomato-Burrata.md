# Tomato and Burrata Sandwich

## Ingredients for 2 servings
### Roasted tomatoes
    300 g           cherry tomatoes
    6               garlic cloves, whole
    2 sprigs        rosemary
    4 tbsp          olive oil
                    salt

### Pesto
    1 bunch         basil (approx. 30 g)
    2 cloves        garlic
    30 g            pine nuts
    30 g            Parmesan
    5 tbsp          olive oil
                    salt

### Also
    1               baguette
    1               burrata (125 g)
    1 handful       rocket
    1 tbsp          chilli oil
    1 tbsp          balsamic glaze
    2 tbsp          olive oil

## Preparation
**Roasted tomatoes:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C conventional (top and bottom heat) or 180 °C fan. Put the cherry tomatoes into a baking dish together with the whole garlic cloves, drizzle generously with olive oil, salt them and mix. Add the rosemary sprigs and roast everything for 25-30 minutes until the tomatoes burst and are soft.

**Pesto:** Pound the garlic with salt in a mortar, add the pine nuts and keep pounding. Add the basil leaves and olive oil and work them into a coarse paste. Finally stir in freshly grated Parmesan and bring it to the consistency you like with olive oil.

**Bread:** Cut the baguette open lengthways, drizzle both cut sides with olive oil and toast them cut side down in a pan until golden brown.

**Assembly:** Spread the bottom half thickly with pesto and top it with rocket. Place the burrata on it, cut it open and spread it out. Drizzle with chilli oil, add the roasted tomatoes and finish with balsamic glaze. Put the lid on, press down and cut the sandwich in half.

## Duration
1 h
