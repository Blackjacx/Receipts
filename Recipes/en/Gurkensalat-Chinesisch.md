# Chinese Cucumber Salad

## Ingredients
    1               cucumber
    1 pinch         sugar
    2 tbsp          rice vinegar (already sweet)
    1 tbsp          soy sauce
    1 tbsp          sesame oil
    1 clove         garlic
    1 tsp           chilli flakes
    1 tsp           sesame seeds
                    coriander

## Preparation
Halve the cucumber lengthways and crush it, e.g. with a broad knife, so that the fibres break open and the cucumber soaks up the dressing better later. Now cut the cucumber into pieces, put it into a bowl, salt it well and mix. While the cucumber draws water, make a dressing from the remaining ingredients and make sure the sugar dissolves well. Now pour off the cucumber water that has formed and mix the cucumber with the dressing.

## Duration
10 min
