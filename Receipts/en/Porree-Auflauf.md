# Leek Bake

## Ingredients
    750 g           potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
    750 g           leeks, trimmed and cut into thick slices
    250 g           ham, cooked, diced
    30 g            butter
    30 g            flour
    250 ml          milk
    250 ml          stock
    125 g           cheese (Emmental), grated
    1 tsp           caraway seeds, whole
                    salt
                    pepper, ground
                    butter for the baking dish
                    salted water for blanching

## Preparation
Blanch the potato slices in boiling salted water for 5-7 minutes, take them out, spread them in the buttered baking dish and scatter the diced ham over the potato slices. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, slowly add the milk and stock while stirring, add the cheese and season with salt, pepper and caraway. Spread half of the sauce over the ham. Blanch the leek slices in boiling salted water for 3 minutes, take them out, spread them over the sauce and pour the remaining sauce over them. Bake at 200°C for 30 minutes.

## Duration
60 min

## Servings
4
