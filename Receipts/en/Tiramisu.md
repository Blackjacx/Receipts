# Tiramisu

## Ingredients for 6 servings
    2           bananas
    2           eggs
    3 tbsp      sugar
    500 g       mascarpone
    75 ml       espresso
    2 cl        amaretto
    200 g       ladyfingers
                cocoa powder, unsweetened

## Preparation
Separate the yolks from the whites. Beat the yolks, sugar and amaretto until creamy. Carefully stir in the mascarpone. Whisk the egg whites until stiff and fold them in. Purée the bananas and stir them into the cream as well.

Soak the ladyfingers in espresso or brush them with it, as you prefer.

Line a baking dish with the soaked ladyfingers and spread half of the mascarpone cream over them. Now add another layer of ladyfingers and mascarpone cream.

Dust the tiramisu with cocoa, cover it with cling film and let it set in the fridge overnight. Dust with cocoa again before serving.

## Duration
15 min preparation time<br />
8 h waiting time
