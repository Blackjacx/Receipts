# Solyanka

## Ingredients
                    margarine for frying
    3               onions
    250 g           Fleischwurst or Jagdwurst (German pork sausage)
    1               pepper, red
    1 jar           Letscho (Hungarian pepper and tomato stew)
    1 tin           tomatoes, diced
    1 tin           tomato juice
                    salt
                    pepper
                    sweet paprika
                    hot paprika

## Preparation
Sweat the diced onion and pepper in the margarine. Then add the sausage, also diced, and fry it without letting it brown. Add the jar of Letscho and the diced tomatoes. If needed, top everything up with tomato juice. Season with salt, pepper, sweet paprika and hot paprika.

## Duration
approx. 40 min
