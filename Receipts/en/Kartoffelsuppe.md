# Potato Soup

## Ingredients
    1 kg            floury potatoes
    2 litres        vegetable stock
    500 g           carrots
    1/4 bulb        celeriac
    1               onion
    125 g           smoked bacon, diced
    1/2 bunch       spring onions
    2 tablespoons   oil or margarine
                    salt
                    pepper
                    marjoram to taste

## Preparation
Peel the potatoes, dice them small, cook them in the stock, let them cool a little and purée them. If the consistency is too thick, add a little more stock to taste. Season with marjoram, salt and pepper.

Meanwhile, peel the carrots, onions and celeriac and dice them very small. Put the diced vegetables with the fat and the bacon into a deep frying pan and braise them over low heat until cooked (takes about 30 min). Stir well now and then and make sure the onions don't brown. Finally season with salt and pepper.

Add the cooked vegetables to the puréed potato soup. Slice the spring onions into rings and add them just before serving.

## Duration
1 h
